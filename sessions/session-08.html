Session 08

The Party

A warm party, a hard boundary set, and a document delivered into the wrong hands — almost. Wren's warning lands. The Ash Compact's move on Jared is stopped cold. Scurlock attends his first party in centuries.

Attendance

  • Present: Alaric, Harrow, Brick, Costya
  • Absent: Flint, Victor — their threads this session (the newspaper discovery, the Dirmot confrontation) didn't fire as written and are delayed into the finale.

Before the Party

Wren, and the Fork

Wren tracked a disturbance trail to the Sunken City looking for Victor, met Marge first, and left a sealed letter tucked into Alaric's helmet before she left — from Schieven, informing him a party was already arranged for that Saturday, whether he'd agreed to it or not. Alaric filled the Anya courting clock before Saturday arrived — Fork A: warm party, better cover, Schieven relieved rather than furious.

Talking Jared Down

During downtime, the crew talked Jared out of the Meridian Circle scheme entirely — he uninvited his own handlers before the party even started. Two Ash Compact cultists tried a desperate breach at the rear of the mansion instead. Costya, running overwatch with her hawk, killed both and dumped them in the canal before the Wardens arrived. The Wardens know someone died in Bright Crown that night. They don't know where, or how.

The Party

Schieven's Office

Schieven pulled Alaric aside early, trying to talk him out of "the hobby." Alaric held his ground. Then Jared — cheerful, oblivious, finger-guns and all — blurted that Alaric had already faced down Setarra by name, not a vague "demon." Schieven went very still. The conversation got heated, and landed on a hard boundary: family and marriage come first, and Alaric is done with the demon business. The finale is about to directly violate this.

GM — Schieven's Own Setarra Debt (not known to the PCs)

Years ago, worn down by constantly cleaning up after Jared, Schieven made his own bargain with an arcane contact who turned out to be Setarra — the family business in exchange for "doing his job" on one specific day. That day he found the boiler Jared had left unlocked, locked it properly, and left. It exploded minutes later, killing Cobb and Maren Barrow — Flint's parents. Jared was blamed and broken; their father died of shame; Schieven inherited everything and has spent years quietly shielding Jared out of guilt for something Jared doesn't know happened.

Schieven is now an independent thread tying the Dalmorrians to Setarra, separate from Alaric's. He just heard her name spoken aloud in his own office — live pressure heading into the finale. This is planned to surface at the table via Harker, mid-Session-9. Do not reveal it to players before then.

Anya

She confronted Alaric on whether any of it is real. He told her the truth, including Setarra's name. She held steady — relieved it'll be over soon — but drew her own hard line: her parents, and the Hive, can never know.

Scurlock Attends

Dressed to the nines, out of his element but self-possessed. Old blood wary, new blood fascinated. He toasted "re-initiating good relations between House Scurlock and Dalmorre" with a bottle bearing the old House Dalmorre crest — and incidentally rescued Alaric from the Quellin aunt's interrogation.

Brick

Let Jared into Schieven's office against orders — which set up the Setarra blurt. Botched a flirt-block on the Quellin aunt. Flipped a poker table to escape a losing hand, then deflected the furious nobles by offering them a meeting with Schieven, "sent by Alaric." Also watched condensation flow upward on a champagne glass.

Harrow's Infiltration

Got a hawk-delivered warning from Costya mid-job. Jared unknowingly gave him cover by pulling the household staff into a story-and-wine break. Ran into the city's "wrongness" directly — a door reading locked that wasn't. Encountered Dirmot Eckardd doing ghost-ley-line surveying under the estate — a tense standoff, no violence. Dirmot fled, leaving behind strange line-graph "ghost river" paperwork, now held for Victor to decode.

Harrow retrieved both the Dalmorrian document and the newspaper clipping about the Boiler Works fire from the archive — meaning Harrow, not Flint, currently holds the article naming Jared in his own parents' deaths. The emotional reveal to Flint is delayed, and will land harder once he's present.
