Harker

Runs the Dalmorrian warehouse. Does not want to see Alaric.

Who He Is

  • Connected to Jared — his legitimate son; runs the Dalmorrian family warehouse
  • Not set to inherit much beyond the job — the Dalmorrian name and estate run through Schieven's line, not Jared's, so Harker does the labor without the standing
  • Competent and clearly doing the actual work of the household while Jared pursues his new interests
  • Hostile to Alaric — the reason is not yet known to Alaric, though the crew learned it in Session 9 (see below)
  • First encountered in Session 7: attempted to turn Alaric away at the warehouse door, then got called off to handle warehouse business

What the Crew Has Seen

Session 7 — Warehouse Encounter

Alaric arrived at the Dalmorrian warehouse to speak with Jared. Harker intercepted him at the door. The reception was cold — not a formal refusal, but unmistakably unwelcoming. He attempted to turn Alaric away. Before the situation could develop further, something in the warehouse required his attention and he had to withdraw. Alaric proceeded inside and found Jared.

Alaric did not understand the hostility. He may not have registered it fully, or he may be missing something about the history between them.

The Hostility

What’s Behind It — Resolved Session 9

Harker is Jared’s legitimate son, but not set to inherit much beyond the job of running the warehouse — the Dalmorrian name and estate run through Schieven’s line, not Jared’s. Two things stack on top of that structural unfairness:

  • Favoritism: Jared visibly prefers Alaric to his own son — the two of them are, in Harker’s words, “so damn alike.” Harker does the real work; Alaric gets the warmth.
  • The deeper betrayal: Schieven — Alaric’s father — is the one who spent years quietly cleaning up after Jared (correcting purchase orders, covering for him, locking up equipment) while Harker did the same thankless work from below. Harker only learned from Setarra that Schieven knew what was going to happen to Jared and didn’t stop it — reframing decades of Schieven’s “cleanup” as complicity. Harker delivers this to Alaric directly during the Beat 10 monologue on the Session 9 GM page.
Setarra’s Interest

If Jared becomes unavailable or inaccessible, Harker is a viable fallback — he is part of the Dalmorrian household, likely carries the bloodline, and is already doing the practical work of the family. He may be a more competent target than Jared in some ways, though also harder to flatter or manipulate. A different approach would be required.

Connections

  • Jared — his father; Harker covers for him and runs the actual household operations
  • Alaric — hostile relationship; reason unresolved
  • The Dalmorrian household — he is the person actually keeping it functional
